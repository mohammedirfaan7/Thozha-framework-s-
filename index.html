Our Story

Made with love, for the people you love

Thonza Frame Work is a local shop dedicated to one idea: your photographs deserve more than a phone screen. Every frame, lamp, clock and keychain that leaves our shop is assembled and personalised by hand.

From wedding portraits to a baby's first photo, we help you hold onto the moments that matter — in a form you can keep on a shelf, a wall, or a keyring.
